Guide to rating

Goal: would a good gene–disease search show this paper?

  • 2 = yes, the paper we want.
  • 1 = a near miss.
  • 0 = no.

Grade from the abstract alone to keep the comparison fair. Looking up disease info is fine.

2
Evidence. The abstract reports evidence that this gene (or a homolog) is involved in this disease, a synonym or a subtype, including in a model of the disease.
1
Related or mention. The abstract discusses both the gene and the disease, but either indirectly or in a related disease (see below).
0
Unrelated. Either an unrelated disease, the gene alone (e.g. basic research), or not this gene at all.
U
Unsure. If you can't decide 2, 1, or 0. Optionally leave a note before rating.

Same disease, or related? This disease by another name, a subtype of a broader disease, or the disease described only by its symptoms: all count as the same disease. Rate a broader disease as 1 or 0 unless the study includes this disease. Use your own scientific judgement to decide whether two different diseases are related, e.g. epilepsy and schizophrenia.
