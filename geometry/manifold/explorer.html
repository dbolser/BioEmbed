How to use it

Every point here is a direction in a 384-number space where each paper is an arrow. Close arrows mean similar papers.

Axes were found three ways: directions papers vary along inside nearly every topic (W: e.g. gene mapping ↔ drug treatment), directions that separate topics (B), and the shared time direction (older → newer). Analogy themes (A) are between-topic moves that recur across the map, e.g. "X in cancer → X".

Results always name real topics, concepts and papers near the point. "Nearest paper" says how close the point still is to a real paper: if it drops, the walk has left the literature.
